# HomeKind privacy statement

Published at <https://wattkind.ai/homekind/privacy/> (founder-reviewed, live
since October 2026). The published page in `red3112/WattKind-AI`
(`homekind/privacy/index.html`) is the canonical copy; this file is the source
paragraph and must be kept in step with it. The live page also states that the
iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup may include HomeKind's data, that the
statement will be updated before any version sends data off the phone, and gives
`support@wattkind.ai` as the contact.

**Android (to add to the live page with the Play release):** the same sentence
applies to Android's own device backup. The Android app leaves `allowBackup`
on, so Google's backup of the phone may include HomeKind's records and
attachments, under the person's own Google account and their phone's backup
settings, exactly as iCloud backup does on iPhone. HomeKind itself still sends
nothing anywhere.

---

HomeKind stores everything you enter — device names, rooms, purchase and
warranty dates, and any photos or manuals you attach — on your phone, and
nowhere else. It sends nothing to WattKind AI or to anyone else. There is no
account and no sign-in, because HomeKind has nothing to attach an account to.
If you export a backup, that backup is a file you choose where to save, and
it stays under your control from that point on — we never see it. If you have
a question about this, you can reach us through wattkind.ai.
