# Draft — founder review required

This is a draft of the short privacy statement for `wattkind.ai/homekind/privacy`.
It has not been reviewed by the founder and should not be published as-is.

---

HomeKind stores everything you enter — device names, rooms, purchase and
warranty dates, and any photos or manuals you attach — on your phone, and
nowhere else. It sends nothing to WattKind AI or to anyone else. There is no
account and no sign-in, because HomeKind has nothing to attach an account to.
If you export a backup, that backup is a file you choose where to save, and
it stays under your control from that point on — we never see it. If you have
a question about this, you can reach us through wattkind.ai.
